Published on: 7/14/2026

How does ginger contribute to weight loss, and what is the recommended intake?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ginger may support weight loss by boosting metabolism, reducing fat absorption, curbing appetite, and improving digestion. Studies suggest 1–3 grams of ginger daily—consumed as tea, fresh root, or supplements—can aid modest weight reduction when paired with a balanced diet and exercise. However, ideal intake varies by individual, and ginger isn't a standalone solution.

Because weight changes can stem from many underlying causes—hormonal imbalances, metabolic conditions, or lifestyle factors—it's worth understanding what's really driving your symptoms before starting any regimen.
