Supreme Court Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg is 85 years old, but she shows no signs of slowing down thanks to her famously challenging workout.

Stephen Colbert decided to try out her exercise routine on The Late Show, and now everyone can see why she's still able to keep up both in and out of the court.

Here's How the Workout Went Down

Led by her personal trainer, Bryant Johnson, the Supreme Court justice started her workout with some stretches. She made sure to wear her Super Diva shirt, which Johnson sells to put money toward encouraging women who have a cancer diagnosis to workout more.

Colbert tried to pump her up by playing C+C Music Factory's "Gonna Make You Sweat," but she was not having it. "I would never, never exercise to that noise," she said.

The RBG Workout

Johnson has also released a book about Ginsburg's workout so followers can try it out at home. According to The Guardian, Ginsburg started working out with Johnson in 1999.

"I am often consumed by the heavy lifting Supreme Court judging entails, [and I am] reluctant to cease work until I am sure I’ve got it right," Ginsburg writes in the book, according to The Guardian. "But when time comes to meet with Bryant, I leave off and join him at the gym for justices. The hour-long routine he has developed suits me to a T."

According to Johnson's website, Ginsburg works out with him twice a week. "[She does] full-strength planks, push-ups, chest and shoulder presses, bicep and leg curls, one-legged squats and knee raises,” Johnson told the Seattle Times. “Pull downs, cable-rows. Inner- and outer-thigh. Glute work."
