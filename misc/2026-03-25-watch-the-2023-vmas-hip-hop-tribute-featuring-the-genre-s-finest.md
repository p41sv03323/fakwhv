But last night's awards show, hosted by Nicki Minaj, also featured an homage to hip-hop specifically, marking the genre's 50th anniversary with an ensemble performance from some of the most highly regarded rappers in the game.

Grandmaster Flash and the Furious Five opened the 10-minute tribute with a rendition of "The Message," and they were then joined by Doug E. Fresh and the Get Fresh Crew who performed "The Show."

Minaj, the MC of the evening, then hit the stage to sing "Itty Bitty Piggy" and "Red Ruby da Sleeze," before cuing up an entrance from Lil Wayne, who was her mentor at Young Money, and performed "A Milli."

The next artist to rock up was LL Cool J, in his first VMAs performance since the late '90s. Entering to the sound of police sirens, he mashed up "I'm Bad" and "Mama Said Knock you Out," and then introduced hip-hop legend DMC (of Run-DMC fame). "Fifty years of hip-hop, it's time for one third of the group that knocked down walls for all of us," he said.

LL joined DMC in singing a medley of Run-DMC hits, and the tribute ended with every performer rejoining them on-stage for their best known track, "Walk This Way."
