About

Carly Schuna is a professional freelance writer and editor with more than two decades of publishing experience and more than a decade of experience as an athlete and coach in the fitness and health industry. Schuna is a cofounder of the Madison Circus Space, a nonprofit circus arts organization in Madison, Wisconsin, where she coaches and trains.

She has a personal fitness training certification from Monkey Bar Gym, a Level 2 wheel gymnastics coaching certification from the International Wheel Gymnastics Federation, aerial hoop teacher training, Nimble Arts aerial instructor training, Pilates for Aerialists instructor training from Twisted Bodies, and five national championship titles from USA Wheel Gymnastics.
