Sweatpants and hoodies get all the hype when it comes to loungewear. And, sure, there's room in all of our closets for some full-on comfy fits. But if you feel hot in hoodies and constricted in pants—or you're just looking to expand your loungewear repertoire—look to something a little shorter and just as comfy: the sweat short.

Yes, comfy sweat shorts are extremely '90s weightlifting and yes, that is exactly what makes sweat shorts so stylish. You see, men's fashion is in this time of embracing the old, the clunky, and the little-too-retro. Dad sneakers? Yes. Tropical tourist button-front shirts? Of course. Sweat shorts? You bet your comfy quads they are.

You have options here when it comes to what they should look like, how they should fit, and what you can wear them with. There's a sweat short for every price range and a style for every level of sweat short skill, from the very basic to the fully tie-dyed. For the fit, it's important that you don't overthink it. Sweat shorts are one of those items of clothing that require you don't try so hard. They are sweat shorts, after all. The best bet is to go for a sweat short that hits a little above the knee, but more advanced sweat short-wearers can go as above or below as they please.
