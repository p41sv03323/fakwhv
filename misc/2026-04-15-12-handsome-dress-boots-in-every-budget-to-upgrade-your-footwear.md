FRANKLY, IF we can wear dad sneakers or slippers to black-tie events, we absolutely will not object. But in reality, it’s not so much about whether we can or can’t, but whether we’d look right doing so. So, when it comes to formal footwear, we understandably default to dress shoes to ground our fits of blazers and dress pants. But should you want to look really elevated—like an A-lister making an appearance on the red carpet—a handsome pair of dress boots will do even better.

The best dress boots for men often have a solid construction, fine leather or suede outsole, and a rubber heel; they often boast a slim, streamlined profile, and never look out of style. Often worn with a suit, the best dress boots can also get you through sleet and snow with ease, dressed up for the office or date night, or dressed down with chinos.
