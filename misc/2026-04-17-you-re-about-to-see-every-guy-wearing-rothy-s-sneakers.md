There’s nothing worse than that uncomfortable break-in period for new leather shoes. Though performance sneakers rarely come with this issue, its futuristic design and silhouette unattractively contrast more formal attire. The genius of the new RS01 sneaker is its entirely knit upper, created using Rothy’s innovative 3D-knitting techniques that give it a sleek, timeless look, along with the most comfortable feel. Your feet will easily slide into this sneaker with its flexible, knit sock-like liner—finally, no more awkward, foot-shoving shuffle dance to get on your fresh kicks. And if you’re the kind of guy who loves the bare ankle look with a cropped trouser, it doesn’t get much better than this.

Rothy’s signature knit footwear has already grown a cult-like following with women, so it makes sense the brand would eventually expand to menswear. But it’s not just about comfort and cool style—the brand takes a fully sustainable approach when creating shoes that truly make it a feel-good purchase.

You Don’t Need to Sacrifice Cool Style for Sustainability

Don’t be fooled by Rothy’s less-is-more design ethos—the team’s thoughtful, sustainable approach to footwear takes time, innovative techniques, and a heavy dose of passion.

To create these eco-friendly shoes, the brand owns it’s factories and equipment, allowing them to have more control of the over environmental waste that can be caused by excess material and overproduction. And speaking of waste, Rothy’s took millions of plastic bottles and turned them into small pellets that becomes the yarn for shoe’s upper, creating a sneaker that has one-fifth of the carbon footprint of sneakers made with virgin materials. With their game-changing machinery, the brand is able to construct these knit uppers to the perfect shape that only creates 1% of material waste. Plus, Rothy’s only uses seven parts to create this sneaker, which decreases waste by 30% compared to standard shoes that use 21 parts.

Living a more sustainable lifestyle isn’t as hard as it may seem when a brand like Rothy’s is disrupting the footwear industry by create quality, eco-friendly product that’s equally stylish and comfortable.

A Fresh Look Is Just a Wash Away

If you live an active lifestyle, chances are that your shoes can get scuffed and marked up by your daily adventures pretty quickly, but that shouldn’t mean you have to treat your footwear like a precious piece of art. Rothy’s gets it—that’s why the brand's cool sneakers are machine washable. Wash-tested 60x to guarantee the perfect fit, was after wash, you can make your go-to sneakers look brand new without having to break a sweat in panic trying different spot treatments. No matter how many spills or splashes come your way, you can chuck them in the washer machine and know your hard-earned money didn’t go to waste. If cost-per-wear value means anything to you, look no further.

Rothy’s RS01 sneakers have truly become the MVP of my sneaker line-up. While I, like most men, figure out how to dress for social occasions again and amp up our workwear wardrobes for a return to the office, there’s no question that I’ll be building out my daily ‘fits around my Rothy’s.
