Foods that Help Hangovers

Is the secret to a hangover cure hidden in food? Certain foods may help ease your symptoms and help rejuvenate your body during a hangover. Some of these foods that help hangovers are:

Eggs – The classic and best hangover breakfast. Eggs are soft and bland food that will help your body get some strength to heal. Just make sure not to eat it with a bunch of oils or fry them with bacon.

Ginger – is known to help settle the stomach.

Toast – Carbohydrates tend to help cure a hangover. It is also bland and will be easy on the stomach.

Chicken soup – this is a staple to help feel better. It helps the body become hydrated and helps to balance sodium levels.

Banana – This will help replenish potassium levels after a night of drinking.

Staying hydrated is the most important during a hangover, and drinking fluids like water, tea, coconut water, and sports drinks is going to benefit you. Be sure to avoid soda and caffeine.

Hangover Foods to avoid

Some foods are best to avoid when you have a hangover. To avoid worsening symptoms or a prolonged hangover, make sure to avoid these foods:

Fried or oily foods – contrary to old wives’ tales saying to eat a burger and fries during a hangover, greasy food can actually cause nausea making the hangover get worse.

Excess meat – too much protein during a hangover can cause headaches to feel worse.

Coffee – caffeine is known to dehydrate, which is not a good thing during a hangover. You want the opposite: more hydration!

Alcohol – This one should be obvious, but more alcohol can cause the hangover to be prolonged and further upset the stomach. Avoid the “hair of the dog” advice.
