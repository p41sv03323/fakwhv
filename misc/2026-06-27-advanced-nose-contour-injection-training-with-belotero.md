About this lesson

Enhance your skills in achieving a beautifully refined and balanced nose shape with this advanced training video. Designed for medical professionals, this comprehensive video walks you through the step-by-step process of assessing nasal deviations, deepening frontonasal angles, and restoring volume in key areas using Belotero products. Learn how to select the right product for structural imitation, apply the product with precision, and mold it for natural-looking results. Address specific concerns such as prominent cartilage, wide bone structures, and deep nasal angles with expert-led instruction. This video provides a practical, evidence-based approach to advanced nasal contouring, enabling you to deliver harmonious, personalized results that will exceed patient expectations and elevate your practice.

Techniques to soften the frontonasal angle and restore soft tissue volume for a smoother, natural contour.
