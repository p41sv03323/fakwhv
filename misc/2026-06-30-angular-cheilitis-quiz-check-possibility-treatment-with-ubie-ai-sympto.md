What is Angular Cheilitis?

Inflammation of the corners of the lips. Causes include irritants, such as constant contact with saliva or excessive dryness, which then lead to overgrowth of yeast. Rarely it is associated with nutritional deficiencies and immune system disorders.

Typical Symptoms of Angular Cheilitis

Lip sores or erosions

Dry mouth

Lip pain or itch

Lip redness

Pain in the mouth

Skin problems

Redness, bumps, or irritation on the skin

Diagnostic Questions for Angular Cheilitis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are there sores or erosions on your lips?

Are you experiencing a dry mouth?

Are your lips itchy or painful?

Are your lips red?

Do you have pain in your mouth?

Treatment of Angular Cheilitis

Treatment consists primarily of antifungal and cortisone creams. Lip balm and petroleum jelly can keep the lips moist.
