What is Benign Mucosal Tumors?

These tumors arise from the moist, inner lining of some organs and body cavities (such as the nose, mouth, lungs, and stomach). The exact cause is unknown. These are typically not life-threatening, and the prognosis is good.

Treatment of Benign Mucosal Tumors

No specific treatment is required for benign tumors unless there are symptoms, in which case surgical removal may be required.
