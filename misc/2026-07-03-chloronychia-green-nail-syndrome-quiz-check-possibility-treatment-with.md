What is Chloronychia (Green Nail Syndrome)?

This is a nail infection that causes greenish discoloration. It is caused by bacteria that thrive in damp environments.

Treatment of Chloronychia (Green Nail Syndrome)

Keeping the nail dry is essential, as well as trimming any detached nail. Antibiotic creams and tablets may be prescribed to treat the infection.
