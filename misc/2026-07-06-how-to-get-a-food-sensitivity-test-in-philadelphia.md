What is a food sensitivity test?

A food sensitivity test is when you eat food and then measure the response to foods in your body. This can be done with a blood test or sometimes with a skin prick test. A food sensitivity is different from a food allergy because it does not involve the immune system. It’s also important to note that intolerance is different than sensitivity. An intolerance may have symptoms similar to an allergy, but the immune system isn’t involved. Common food sensitivity symptoms include:

Headaches

Bloating

Nausea

Skin irritation
