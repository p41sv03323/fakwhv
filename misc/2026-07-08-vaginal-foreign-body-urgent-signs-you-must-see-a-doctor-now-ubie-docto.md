If you think something is stuck in your vagina, see a doctor right away. Retained objects—such as tampons, condoms, or contraceptive devices—can cause serious complications, including infection, toxic shock syndrome, or tissue damage, if not removed promptly.

Common signs of a retained object include foul-smelling discharge, pelvic pain, unusual bleeding, fever, or discomfort during daily activities. A healthcare provider can safely remove the object and check for signs of infection.
