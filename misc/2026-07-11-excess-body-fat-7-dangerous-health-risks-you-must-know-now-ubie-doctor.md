Excessive body fat, often referred to as obesity, is linked to several health conditions. One major health issue is diabetes. Obesity can cause changes in how the body uses insulin, leading to type 2 diabetes. This happens because too much fat can make it harder for the body to control blood sugar levels.

Other health problems linked to obesity include metabolic diseases. These are conditions that affect how the body uses energy and processes food. Obesity can lead to high blood pressure, high cholesterol, and heart disease. It can also cause problems with breathing and increase the risk of certain cancers.

In summary, having too much body fat can affect many parts of the body and lead to serious health problems.
