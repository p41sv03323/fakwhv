How does the survival rate of prostate cancer vary by age group?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Prostate cancer survival rates are strongly linked to age at diagnosis. In general, younger men have higher survival rates than older men, while survival rates tend to decline as age increases. This pattern is influenced by factors such as overall health, how aggressive the cancer is at diagnosis, and the body's ability to tolerate treatment.
