What home treatments are effective for diarrhea related to COVID-19?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

COVID-19-related diarrhea can often be managed at home with probiotics, hydration, and rest. Probiotics help restore healthy gut bacteria, while home-based treatments like electrolyte replacement and a bland diet ease symptoms. Telemedicine offers convenient access to healthcare professionals who can guide your recovery and flag when symptoms need in-person care.

Because diarrhea can stem from many causes beyond COVID-19—including infections, medications, or underlying conditions—it's important to understand what's actually driving your symptoms before choosing a treatment.
