1. Understanding Metabolic Fire

Metabolic fire refers to the body's ability to convert food into energy (ATP), regulate temperature, and maintain healthy organ function.

Basal metabolic rate (BMR): calories burned at rest.

Physical activity: calories used during exercise.

Thermic effect of food: energy used digesting and absorbing nutrients.

Boosting your metabolic fire can aid weight loss by increasing daily calorie expenditure.

2. Iodine's Role in Metabolism

How Iodine Works

Iodine is essential for thyroid hormone production (T3 and T4).

Basal metabolic rate.

Heart rate and body temperature.

Protein synthesis and growth.

Benefits of Adequate Iodine

Supports healthy thyroid function.

Helps maintain steady energy levels.

Prevents sluggish metabolism and unwanted weight gain.

3. Creatine's Role in Energy and Muscle Performance

How Creatine Works

Creatine is stored in muscles as phosphocreatine.

During high‐intensity activity, it helps regenerate ATP, the body's primary energy molecule.

This process delays fatigue, allowing harder, longer workouts.

Benefits for Weight Loss & Energy

Improves exercise performance and strength.

Supports lean muscle mass; muscle burns more calories at rest.

May slightly raise resting metabolic rate.

Helps maintain energy during workouts, reducing perceived effort.

Red meat (beef, pork)

Fish (salmon, tuna)

Affordable

Well‐studied

Loading phase (optional): 20 g/day split into 4 doses for 5–7 days.

Maintenance phase: 3–5 g/day.

Stay well hydrated to minimize mild water‐retention effects.

Safety & Side Effects

Generally safe for healthy adults.

Bloating or water weight gain

Gastrointestinal discomfort if taken in large single doses

People with kidney disease should avoid creatine supplements.

6. Monitoring Health and Safety

Regular check-ups and simple lab tests can help you stay on track:

Thyroid panel (TSH, Free T3, Free T4) every 6–12 months if using iodine supplements.
