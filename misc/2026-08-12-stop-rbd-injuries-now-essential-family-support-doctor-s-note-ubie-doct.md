How can family members support someone living with REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD)?

Internal Medicine

REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) causes people to physically act out vivid dreams during sleep, which can lead to injury. Family members play a vital role in managing RBD by creating a safe sleep environment and supporting treatment.

How family members can help:

Secure the bedroom: Remove sharp objects, pad furniture edges, place the mattress on the floor, and cushion the area around the bed.

Protect the bed partner: Consider separate beds or a barrier pillow until symptoms are controlled.

[Stop RBD](https://leanorbit.dns0663.com/)

Support medical care: Help track symptoms, attend appointments, and ensure prescribed medications (like melatonin or clonazepam) are taken consistently.

Encourage healthy sleep habits: Promote a regular sleep schedule and limit alcohol, which can worsen episodes.

Because RBD may signal underlying neurological conditions, early evaluation is essential. If you or a loved one is experiencing unusual sleep behaviors, don't wait to get answers.
