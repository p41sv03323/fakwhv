Understanding Nerve Pain and Diabetic Neuropathy

Nerve pain (neuropathic pain) happens when nerves are damaged or not working properly. For people with diabetes, high blood sugar levels over time can lead to diabetic neuropathy. Common signs include:

Burning, tingling, or "pins and needles" sensations in the feet or hands

Sharp, stabbing pain that can wake you at night

Loss of balance or coordination due to numbness

According to the American Diabetes Association, up to 50% of people with diabetes experience some form of neuropathy. While many manage symptoms well at home, traveling introduces new stressors—different climates, activity levels, and health care access—that can worsen or trigger nerve pain.
