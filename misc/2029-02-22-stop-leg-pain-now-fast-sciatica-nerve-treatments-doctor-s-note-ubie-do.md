Explanation

Pain that travels from the buttocks down the leg can be caused by conditions like piriformis syndrome or sciatica. Piriformis syndrome happens when the piriformis muscle in the buttocks irritates the sciatic nerve. Treatments include stretching exercises, physical therapy, and sometimes injections to reduce inflammation and pain.

Sciatica is another condition where pain travels along the sciatic nerve, which runs from the lower back down the legs. Medications like gabapentin and NSAIDs can be used to help relieve the pain. Gabapentin works by calming the nerves that may be causing the pain. NSAIDs work by reducing pain and inflammation.
