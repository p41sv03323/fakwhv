Explanation

Myasthenia gravis is a condition that affects how muscles work. It often starts with muscle weakness that gets worse with activity and improves with rest.

Common Early Signs:

Eye Problems: People may notice droopy eyelids or double vision.

Face and Throat Issues: There might be trouble with chewing, swallowing, or speaking clearly.

These symptoms happen because the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the places where nerves connect to muscles. This makes it hard for muscles to work properly.
