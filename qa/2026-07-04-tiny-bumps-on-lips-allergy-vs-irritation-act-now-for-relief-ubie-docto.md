Tiny bumps on the lips are often caused by allergic reactions or irritation from common ingredients such as peppermint, alcohol, or fragrances found in lip balms, toothpaste, and foods. These bumps may appear suddenly and can be accompanied by redness, itching, or mild swelling. Identifying and avoiding the trigger usually resolves the issue, but persistent or worsening symptoms may signal a more serious underlying condition.

Because lip bumps can stem from many causes—ranging from minor irritation to infections or other health concerns—it's important to understand what's really behind your symptoms before deciding on treatment.
