What is the gold standard test for diagnosing sleep apnea?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Polysomnography, an overnight sleep study performed in a specialized sleep laboratory, is the gold standard test for diagnosing sleep apnea. This comprehensive test monitors brain activity, breathing patterns, oxygen levels, heart rate, and body movements to accurately identify sleep-disordered breathing.

Polysomnography is considered the gold standard for diagnosing sleep apnea. This test is usually done overnight in a sleep lab, where various body functions are monitored while you sleep.

What is Polysomnography?

Comprehensive Monitoring: It records brain waves, oxygen levels in your blood, heart rate, breathing, and eye and leg movements.

In-Lab Environment: Conducted in a controlled setting, ensuring accurate and detailed results.
