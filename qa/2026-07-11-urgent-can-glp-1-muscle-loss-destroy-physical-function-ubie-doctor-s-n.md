GLP-1 medications, such as Ozempic and Wegovy, can cause muscle loss alongside fat reduction, potentially impacting strength, mobility, and long-term physical function. However, this risk can be minimized through resistance training, adequate protein intake (typically 1.2–1.6g per kg of body weight), and regular monitoring of body composition rather than just weight.

If you're taking a GLP-1 drug—or considering one—and notice changes in strength, energy, or mobility, it's important to understand whether your symptoms are typical side effects or signs of something that needs medical attention.
