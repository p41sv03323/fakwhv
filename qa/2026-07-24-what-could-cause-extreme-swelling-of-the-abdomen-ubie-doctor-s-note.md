Explanation

Extreme swelling of the abdomen can happen due to a few serious conditions. One such condition is abdominal compartment syndrome, which occurs when there is too much pressure inside the abdomen. This can be very dangerous and needs immediate medical attention. Another cause could be acute pancreatitis, where the pancreas becomes inflamed and can lead to bloating and distension of the abdomen.
