What are the potential causes of an itchy urethra in females?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

An itchy urethra in females is often caused by infections, allergies, or irritants affecting the urinary tract and genital area. Common causes include urinary tract infections (UTIs), yeast infections, bacterial vaginosis, sexually transmitted infections (STIs), and irritation from soaps, detergents, or hygiene products. Because the urethra sits close to the vulva and vagina, itching may also stem from nearby conditions like vulvovaginitis or contact dermatitis.

Since symptoms often overlap between conditions, identifying the exact cause on your own can be difficult—and delaying care may allow infections to worsen.
