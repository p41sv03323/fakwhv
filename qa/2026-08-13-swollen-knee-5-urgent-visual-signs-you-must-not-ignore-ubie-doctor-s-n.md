Explanation

When a knee is swollen, doctors often see that the knee looks puffed up and bigger compared to a normal knee. This puffiness happens because extra fluid builds up inside the joint. You might notice that the area around the kneecap or the upper part of your knee looks fuller and more rounded. Sometimes the skin over the swollen part can appear stretched and shiny because it is being pushed out from the extra liquid. In some cases, the swollen knee may also show uneven contours, meaning one part of the knee looks more bulged than another. It is important to remember that people might feel that their knee is swollen even if it does not look very swollen to others. This difference between what you feel and what is seen is something that has been noticed in research. If you're experiencing knee swelling along with other symptoms, you can use our free AI symptom checker to help identify potential causes. Look for a knee that appears larger than the other, with a noticeable bulge especially in the soft tissue areas, to identify visual signs of swelling.
