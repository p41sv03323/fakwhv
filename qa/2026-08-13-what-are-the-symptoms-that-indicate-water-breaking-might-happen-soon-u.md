Explanation

Some women feel signs that their water might break soon even before it fully happens. You might feel more pressure or fullness in your lower belly and pelvic area, as if your body is getting ready for the baby. Another sign can be a small, gentle leak of clear, watery fluid that isn’t like the normal thicker or white discharge you might usually see. You might also have light, irregular contractions or a feeling of gentle cramping. These sensations can indicate that the amniotic sac is starting to thin or that small tears are happening. While every pregnancy is different, these early signs can be the body’s way of saying that labor is getting closer. If you feel any of these symptoms, it’s a good idea to reach out to your healthcare provider. They can check your body and help you know if your water is about to break, so that you can get ready for the next steps in your labor and delivery process.
