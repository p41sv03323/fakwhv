When a period stops suddenly, it could be due to several factors, and pregnancy is one possibility. According to research, pregnancy can occur if sexual intercourse happens near the time of ovulation. Ovulation typically happens about halfway through the menstrual cycle, and this is when an egg is released from the ovary.

Here are some key points:

Timing of Sex and Ovulation: If sex occurs near ovulation, there is a higher chance of pregnancy. This is because the egg is available to be fertilized by sperm.

Implantation and Pregnancy: After fertilization, the fertilized egg, or conceptus, must implant in the uterus for a pregnancy to continue. This process can sometimes cause light bleeding, which might be mistaken for a period.
