About the Symptom

Axillary pain, or pain under armpit, is a painful sensation in one or both armpits. The axilla refers to the armpit, and this armpit pain can be pressing, sharp, or dull, typically caused by muscle strain or swollen lymph nodes. Pain under left armpit or pain under right armpit in females may also be caused by hormonal changes.
