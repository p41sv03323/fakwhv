About the Symptom

Ocular trauma is any injury to the eye, eyelid, or surrounding bone around the eye. Injuries can be blunt or penetrating and can lead to severe vision loss. Injuries may require surgery and close follow up with an eye care professional.

Doctor's Diagnostic Questions

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this symptom:

Did you injure your eyes?

How long ago did you hit/poke your eye(s)?
