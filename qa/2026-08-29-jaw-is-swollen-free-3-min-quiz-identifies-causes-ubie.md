About the Symptom

Jaw swelling can be caused by a lump or swelling on or near the jaw, making it look fuller than usual. It can be accompanied by pain or redness of the skin. Jaw swelling can be due to underlying infection or inflammation and even in some cases tumors or masses. Jaw swelling that persists should be evaluated by a medical professional.
