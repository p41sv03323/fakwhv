Explanation

Itching in the urinary tract, especially in men, is often related to urethritis, which means the tube that carries urine gets inflamed. This inflammation can happen because of infections—bacteria sometimes cause these infections, and in some cases, sexually transmitted germs are the culprits. When the urethra is infected, it may cause itchiness along with other symptoms like burning when you pee, pain, or even a little discharge. Aside from infections, irritation can also be caused by chemicals in personal care products, soaps, or even through physical irritation from items like catheters or other medical devices. In some instances, injuries or trauma to the urethra can lead to similar symptoms. It's important to also look out for other signs such as increased urge to urinate, pain, or any unusual discharge.
