# How to Treat Severe Gastritis

13 May How to Treat Severe Gastritis

Gastritis is a collective term for a group of conditions that cause inflammation of the stomach lining. This inflammation often results from being infected with the bacterium that causes most stomach ulcers, regularly taking certain pain relievers, or drinking too much alcohol.

Acute gastritis occurs suddenly, while chronic gastritis develops over time. Because severe gastritis can cause stomach ulcers and an increased risk of developing stomach cancer, it is important to properly treat gastritis.

Causes of Severe Gastritis

Some causes of severe gastritis are more common, and otters are less common.

Common causes of severe gastritis include bacterial infections, excessive use of NSAIDs, and stress.

Most cases of gastritis caused by a bacterial infection result from infection with Helicobacter pylori bacteria, which break down the protective lining of the stomach and cause inflammation. Stress from a sudden, severe illness or injury (such as severe burns and brain injuries) can bring on gastritis.

Less common causes of gastritis include autoimmune disease and excessive alcohol consumption. If you have an autoimmune disease, your immune system may attack healthy cells in your stomach lining. Chronic alcohol use can erode and irritate the lining of your stomach.

Severe Gastritis Treatment

Medication options to treat severe gastritis include antibiotics, histamine (H2) blockers, and proton pump inhibitors. Antibiotics are used to treat gastritis that is caused by H. pylori infection. H2 blockers and proton pump inhibitors reduce the amount of stomach acid produced during digestion, which gives the stomach lining a chance to heal.

Lifestyle changes that can help manage symptoms include dietary changes and stress reduction techniques.

Complications of Severe Gastritis

There are several potential complications of severe gastritis, such as:

If you experience any complications of severe gastritis, you should seek medical help promptly so your condition does not worsen.

Prevention of Severe Gastritis

Preventative measures for severe gastritis include:

Avoiding triggers

Limiting alcohol and NSAID use

Maintaining a healthy diet and lifestyle

Because bacterial infection can cause gastritis, it is important that you practice good hygiene, such as washing your hands after you use the restroom.
