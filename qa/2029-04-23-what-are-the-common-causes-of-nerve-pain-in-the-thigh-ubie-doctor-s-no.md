Nerve pain in the thigh is commonly linked to a condition known as meralgia paresthetica. This condition occurs when the lateral femoral cutaneous nerve, which supplies sensation to the outer thigh, is compressed.

Causes of Compression:

Tight Clothing: Wearing tight belts or clothing can squeeze the nerve.

Obesity: Excess weight can increase pressure on the nerve.

Pregnancy: Changes in body shape and weight during pregnancy may lead to nerve compression.

Injury or Trauma: Direct injury to the thigh area can also cause nerve compression.
