Isometric exercises where you contract muscles without moving the joint

Eccentric strengthening exercises that focus on slow, controlled movements

Gentle stretching of the forearm muscles and tendons

These exercises help reduce pain and improve function by strengthening the muscles, tendons, and joints around the elbow. For exercises tailored to your specific needs, seek evaluation and guidance from a physical therapist.
